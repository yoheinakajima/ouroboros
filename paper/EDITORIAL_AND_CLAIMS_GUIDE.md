# Editorial and claims guide

Status: revised after isolated Round 1 review
Version: 1.3
Date: 2026-08-06

Change note for version 1.3: outcome claims now name the evaluated object as a
substrate-proposal-acceptance-interface configuration. Cold and state-hidden
ablation are recorded as actor-visible-equivalent in this implementation; the
frozen labeled-ablation rule is preserved alongside pooled-six and
leave-one-out sensitivities. Model coding is a cross-model consistency check,
one coder is disclosed as the proposal-generating model, and compact-package
reproducibility is separated from the pending complete trace archive.

## Paper identity

Working title:

> When Self-Modification Becomes Memory: An Audited Comparison of Three
> Self-Improving Agent Substrates

Central thesis:

> The interface and acceptance mechanism are part of the self-improving
> system.

The paper is an audited measurement study. It does not claim reliable
recursive self-improvement or rank the three substrates by capability.
Outcome comparisons concern recorded substrate-proposal-acceptance-interface
configurations; retained-state properties may still use substrate names.

## Narrative spine

The main paper follows one sequence:

1. Recursive self-improvement is a chain of empirically distinct constructs.
2. The three studied substrates exposed broad native modification surfaces.
3. Their shared reflection and governance layer converted those surfaces
   primarily into retained textual guidance.
4. Expression profiles reveal which retained changes could reach later actors.
5. Equivalent duplicates reveal how much apparent change can arise from
   outcome variability under byte-identical requests.
6. Receipts invalidate both favorable and unfavorable causal anecdotes.
7. The study yields reusable instruments for stronger future claims.

Every main section should leave the reader with one adoptable instrument.
Detailed benchmark context, per-task behavior, robustness checks, timeouts,
and provenance belong in appendices.

## Required terms

- **retention** is the first construct in the recursive-improvement chain.
  **Self-modification** describes the system's update activity.
- **expression bottleneck** names the restriction between retained state and
  what a later actor can access.
- **expression profile** has five dimensions: storage reach,
  eligible-payload reach, semantic-unit reach, selection adaptivity, and
  execution reach. Selection adaptivity asks whether selection is conditioned
  on task input; the per-task-to-probe-union gap reports coverage expansion,
  not relevance or benefit. Execution reach contains two sub-questions:
  whether retained code runs automatically and whether an actor-visible
  invocation handle exists.
- **equivalent duplicates** are repeated labels that issue byte-identical
  first requests for the same task.
- **control ladder** names a set of controls organized by nuisance and licensed
  inference. The payload controls are alternatives, not a monotonic ordering.
- **failure-memory stickiness** names persistent over-retrieval of
  failure-derived memory.
- **update-proposal taxonomy** names the coding scheme. The phrase
  **mutation taxonomy** is prohibited in manuscript prose.

Implementation filenames may retain legacy names until a deliberate artifact
migration. Their reader-facing titles and references must use
**update-proposal taxonomy**.

## Claim boundaries

Lead with:

- retention, expression, behavioral mediation, task improvement, and
  recursive improvement are different empirical claims;
- the common interface turned three broad mutation substrates mainly into
  memory-like textual guidance;
- outcome variability and control construction were large enough to explain
  several apparent architecture effects;
- acceptance, retrieval, and expression are part of the self-improving
  system.

Exclude:

- reliable recursive self-improvement;
- a universal auditability-expressivity law;
- causal harm from failure-memory retrieval;
- architecture ranking from cold or cold-ablation labels;
- a separable matched-lineage intervention from the recorded cold-ablation
  label;
- leaderboard superiority from the small external benchmark probes;
- semantic equivalence of the native mutation spaces;
- formal significance from the 15 dependent equivalent-label deltas.

Do not insinuate that the recorded controls overturn external published
effects. Propose direct independent applications of the instruments instead.

## Numerical precision

- A percentage from a binary family with ten or fewer tasks must include its
  fraction and nearby equivalent-duplicate context.
- The 84-item taxonomy must retain an exploratory qualifier until independent
  human reliability is available. The two current model passes assess prompt
  and model sensitivity; one coder generated the proposals it later coded, so
  their agreement is a cross-model consistency check rather than independent
  human inter-rater reliability.
- The proposal layer was homogeneous natural-language guidance. The native
  modification surfaces were not homogeneous, and native Workspace churn
  included non-text artifacts and metadata.
- Exact task-specific equivalent ranges are descriptive controls. The 15
  label-pair deltas are dependent and do not support formal significance.

## Case-study rule

Case studies must trace:

> retained update -> expression -> plausible mechanism -> later behavior

The Workspace/SymPy apparent success fails the mechanistic-transfer test.
The Hybrid/quota-scheduler apparent failure lies inside its exact
equivalent-duplicate range. Together they show that the audit can reject
causal stories in both directions. A replacement negative case is eligible
only if its regression exceeds that task's exact equivalent range.

## Figure requirements

- Figure 1 must name all five constructs: retention, expression, behavioral
  mediation, task improvement, and recursive improvement.
- Figure 3 must be task-level, with the six equivalent-duplicate scores as a
  cloud and evolved arms overlaid. Its caption must say that the empirical
  maximum is based on 15 dependent label-pair deltas and is descriptive rather
  than a formal significance threshold.

## Voice

- Academic, candid, concrete, and claims-first.
- No em dash characters.
- Avoid a single-sentence “not X but Y” construction.
- Do not use “genuinely,” “honestly,” or “actually.”
- Treat prior work as strong evidence for the constructs it measures.
- Avoid grievance language and avoid self-flagellation.

## Reviewer-1 stress test

The introduction must pre-answer these objections:

1. **This is a well-documented failed experiment.**  
   The contribution is the measurement framework and the controls that
   determine which apparent effects survive audit.
2. **This is one lineage, one model, and a self-built harness.**  
   The scope is a within-study diagnostic demonstration, not a population
   estimate or architecture ranking.
3. **The expression bottleneck is self-inflicted.**  
   That is the point of the result: the interface and acceptance mechanism
   are components of the system and can dominate the native substrate.

## Reliability and sequencing

Before taxonomy prose is treated as final:

1. freeze a second outcome-blind coding pass;
2. report raw agreement and chance-corrected agreement field by field;
3. distinguish model-model sensitivity from human inter-rater reliability;
4. adjudicate disagreements only after both original label files are frozen;
5. preserve the packet, original labels, prompts, responses, and any
   adjudication log.

Experiment B remains closed unless a drafted discussion exposes a specific
reviewer threat that requires causal retrieval-harm evidence. Experiment D is
an Ouroboros development project outside this paper.
