# Paper readiness decision

Date: 2026-07-23

## Verdict

There is a paper here, but it is not “three agents achieved recursive
self-improvement.”

The publication-shaped contribution is an audited measurement study of what
lies between visible self-modification and a defensible recursive-improvement
claim. The null result is useful because the receipts expose why seemingly
positive and negative anecdotes are unreliable.

## Strongest contributions, in order

1. **Expression bottleneck and expression profile**  
   Retained bytes, semantic reach, adaptive selection, runtime activation, and
   actor-callable capability are distinct. Minimal's 1.35% storage reach and
   100% semantic reach is the decisive counterexample to a single ratio.

2. **Equivalent-input outcome spread as an audit control**  
   Six labels issued byte-identical first requests per task, yet seven of 19
   tasks varied. Most evolved results remained inside exact task-specific
   ranges. Stable tasks supplied one-sided opportunities, and
   Minimal/scikit-learn was the sole outside-range regression.

3. **Shared reflection form versus architecture-specific gating**  
   Independent outcome-blind Sol and Terra coding passes both found 84/84
   proposals were natural-language, append-only, nonexecutable operational
   guidance with no specified activation. The replicated form result is
   strong. Interpretive fields were less stable and remain exploratory;
   Workspace, Minimal, and Hybrid mainly differed in which proposals they
   accepted and how retained state was later expressed.

4. **Mechanistic memory audit**  
   Hybrid's failure-derived memories were over-retrieved. Within success and
   failure groups, vocabulary breadth predicted retrieval frequency; the
   adjusted failure coefficient was essentially zero. This identifies the
   lexical selection mechanism, not downstream harm.

5. **Receipts that invalidate both directions of anecdote**  
   The Workspace/SymPy “win” lacked mechanistic transfer. The Hybrid/quota
   “loss” was inside the exact equivalent range. The audit cuts against both
   favorable and unfavorable stories.

6. **Direct evidence of an append-only capacity ceiling**  
   Hybrid reached the 64 KB Pack-source policy and failed to integrate three
   consecutive generations despite repair attempts.

7. **A reusable control ladder**  
   Cold floor, matched ablation, opaque sham, token sham, semantic sham,
   structure-preserving sham, equivalent duplicates, and native-interface
   ablation clarify what each experimental claim needs.

## Claims to lead with

- Self-modification, expression, behavioral mediation, task improvement, and
  descendant productivity are different empirical claims.
- The implemented common interface converted three broad mutation substrates
  mainly into memory-like textual guidance.
- Outcome variability and control construction were large enough to explain
  several apparent architecture effects.
- Acceptance, retrieval, and expression are part of the self-improving system,
  not neutral plumbing.

## Claims to exclude

- reliable recursive self-improvement;
- a universal auditability–expressivity law;
- causal harm from failure-memory retrieval;
- architecture ranking from cold or cold-ablation labels;
- leaderboard superiority from ten SWE or six Terminal tasks;
- semantic equivalence of the native mutation spaces;
- formal significance from 15 dependent equivalent-label deltas.
