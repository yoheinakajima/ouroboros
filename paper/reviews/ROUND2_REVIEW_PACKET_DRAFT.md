## Paper-specific context

This is a within-study diagnostic demonstration using three recorded
substrate-proposal-acceptance-interface configurations, one outer model, one
development lineage per configuration, and held-out families of 10, 6, and 3
tasks. The first four propositions in the paper's five-part decomposition are
instrumented. Recursive improvement is framed as a stronger endpoint requiring
descendant-productivity evidence and is not identified here.

The frozen decision rule compared evolved against the configuration's labeled
cold-ablation draw. A source audit establishes that cold and cold ablation are
actor-visible-equivalent in this implementation: cold ablation validates a
lineage hash, then exposes no lineage-derived context, tool, workspace,
instruction, or later protocol difference. The manuscript preserves the
frozen contrast as a decision record and reports pooled-six and
leave-the-matched-ablation-out sensitivities from compact committed data. None
changes the no-uplift decision.

The compact package supports regeneration of the manuscript, figures, score
tables, control sensitivities, audits, and provenance manifest. Complete raw
trajectory traces remain in a separately gated archive pending disclosure
review and deposit. The local ActiveGraph benchmark and Hybrid Pack share
author-built technology; the paper discloses that structural overlap and the
observed result unfavorable to Hybrid.

The two outcome-blind model-coding passes use different model families, but one
coder also generated the proposals it coded. Their agreement is presented as a
cross-model consistency check, not independent human reliability.

The packet does not ask the reviewer to accept author classifications of
external related work as independently verified. The reviewer should flag
claims needing primary-source or specialist verification.

4. **Closest novelty collision:** name the nearest prior concept or work from

5. **Unsupported or overstated claims:** quote or precisely locate each claim,

6. **Issues by severity:** critical, major, and minor. For each issue provide
