## Agreements

1. The strongest contribution is the integrated evidentiary decomposition:
   retention, expression, behavioral mediation, task improvement, and
   recursive improvement, supported by the expression profile,
   equivalent-duplicates protocol, control ladder, and receipts.
2. The nominal state-hidden control collapses to an actor-visible no-context
   draw. The sham is not a matched benefit control. Those facts must govern
   the headline interpretation rather than appear as secondary caveats.
3. The study is a one-lineage diagnostic demonstration, not a population
   estimate or a ranking of native substrate power.
4. The related-work feature matrix and numeric literature claims were not
   independently verified by this review round.
5. Complete trajectory traces remain gated, so the three trace audits are
   illustrative and independently uninspectable from the compact package.
